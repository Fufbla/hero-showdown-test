HERO SHOWDOWN — STORM ROOFTOP UPDATE

This is a changed-files update for the current Hero Showdown game.
Built from Fufbla/hero-showdown-test commit b4014e8f99620d0fcc9bbf074e9767cb2a364a89 (October 25, 2026).

INSTALL
1. Unzip this package first.
2. Open the MAIN page of your hero-showdown-test repository, where index.html, hero-showdown.html and the assets folder already live.
3. Upload the enclosed index.html and hero-showdown.html there, replacing those two existing files.
4. Open the repository's existing assets folder. Upload the enclosed tempest folder into it. The final location must be assets/tempest/, with the six game asset files directly inside that folder.
5. Commit the uploads. Wait for GitHub Pages to finish publishing, then refresh the game. If an older menu remains, reload the page or reopen it in a fresh tab.

Do not upload the ZIP itself as the game. Do not delete your existing assets, harbor, cave, shore, images, or videos folders. You do not need to upload any unchanged files.
The two TXT notes are optional reference files and are not required for gameplay.

PLAY
Versus > Solo / Same Device / Online Co-op > difficulty > hero > villain > STORM ROOFTOP.
The Air Pollution Monster stays in the background as an arena hazard.
It winds up and throws a tornado from either side across the entire rooftop.
Hold BLOCK when it reaches your fighter. A successful block prevents ALL tornado damage, regardless of facing direction.
Jumping, dashing, normal invulnerability, and specials cannot evade the wind. Special movie playback pauses the hazard with the rest of combat.
An unblocked pass removes 18 HP from a hero or 18% of the villain's maximum HP. Each fighter is checked only once per tornado.
Villains can also brace and block; their reaction chance increases with difficulty.
Keyboard: L to block in solo; H for P1 / L for P2 in same-device co-op.
Phone: hold the existing BLOCK button.
Controller: LB / L1 or LT / L2.
First windup begins after 6–9 seconds. Later attacks have a random 9–15 second rest after the previous animation finishes.
The windup lasts 1.9 seconds, the throw 0.48 seconds, and the sweep 3.35 seconds.
