HERO SHOWDOWN - STORY MODE EPISODE 1

Extract this ZIP first. Upload the contents into the existing hero-showdown-autoplay folder in your hero-showdown-test repository, preserving assets/ and videos/. Do not nest another hero-showdown-autoplay folder inside it.

The game entry is hero-showdown.html. Include the new videos/smog-battle.mp4 and assets/images/story-smog-rooftop.webp, videos/reborn.mp4, videos/is-this-the-end.mp4, and the updated assets/js/story-mode.js and assets/js/story-team.js. Story Mode requires development code 1992. Choose a starting hero, watch Episode 1, then face Wastelander in THREE battles. Winning battle 1 automatically plays battle23.mp4 and starts battle 2. Winning battle 2 plays the same clip once more and starts battle 3. Winning battle 3 automatically plays smog-battle.mp4, then starts battle 4 against the original Air Pollution Monster on the supplied rooftop venue. The Air Pollution Monster also has THREE battles, all on the rooftop. Winning its first battle (overall battle 4) plays reborn.mp4 and starts its second battle (overall battle 5). Winning its second battle plays reborn.mp4 again and starts its third battle (overall battle 6). Winning its third battle automatically plays is-this-the-end.mp4 (the supplied isthisdEND?.mp4), then shows To be continued. The ending clip plays once per completed run; finishing or skipping it does not start another battle. Reborn.mp4 does not play after this final win. Defeat retries the current battle; replaying the episode restarts at battle 1 and resets the ending clip for the next completed run. All heroes and the opponent start each battle with fresh health. Retrying any rooftop fight keeps that round, the rooftop venue, and the Air Pollution Monster, without replaying the intermission video.

Tag heroes with Q, the on-screen TAG button, or RT / R2. Each hero keeps her health and special charge when tagged. Downed heroes are skipped; all three must be knocked out to lose. The numeric gate is a local development lock, not server authentication.

For local testing, keep all folders together and open hero-showdown.html. The separate hero-showdown-story-mode.html download embeds the media for single-file testing.
