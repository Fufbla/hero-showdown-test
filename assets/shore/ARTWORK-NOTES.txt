SHORE FRONT ARTWORK

shore-front.jpg is an unchanged copy of the supplied 93802DBD-FB87-4C02-BA8F-4D164EC7FC20.jpeg (1536 x 864). It retains the beach, ocean, Neptune statue, damaged/burning city, sunset and original pollution pools. The renderer shows the full image at the game's existing 16:9 arena ratio.

scorpion-atlas.png is a generated transparent 1536 x 1024 sprite atlas based on the supplied "Super Vee Battles Oil Scorpions" reference. Production brief: preserve the black oily comic-book armor, glowing orange eyes, large pincers, curled stinger and dripping sludge; create 24 readable side-view frames on transparency, arranged as six columns by four rows, all facing left, suitable for the existing Supergirls fighting game.

256 x 256 source cells:
0–5: crawling poses.
6–11: crouch, anticipation, takeoff, airborne tuck/reach, landing.
12–17: pincer/stinger attacks and recovery.
18–23: recoil, crumple, fragmentation, dissolving sludge and splash.
The renderer flips these for the opposite direction, animates their travel continuously, and clips the emergence at the surface of each bubbling puddle.

Original Supergirl designs and all existing hero/low-sweep/uppercut sprite assets are unchanged. The supplied concept reference itself is not a runtime dependency.
