HERO SHOWDOWN — ATOMDUST COMBINED MANUAL UPDATE
October 26, 2026

This package includes Atomdust, Venomis & Thornbear, Sonic Tusk, Styrodog,
Dr. Styro, Wastelander, and the saved Supergirls animation work. Use this ZIP
in place of the earlier animation-update ZIPs.

INSTALL WHEN YOU ARE HOME
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Copy the CONTENTS of GAME-FILES into your existing game folder, beside
   index.html. Keep the assets/animation folder structure. Replace matching
   files and keep the other existing game files.
3. Upload the files yourself, then reload without old cached files.

There are 50 game files: 42 production files and eight optional review files
for Styrodog, Sonic Tusk, Venomis & Thornbear, and Atomdust. README.txt and
DEVELOPMENT contain notes and checks for reference.

ATOMDUST
His original debris artwork now uses an eight-step blended walk, planted
feet during idle breathing, stronger body preparation and follow-through
for his five attacks, and a short transition back to guard. Normal movement
uses ten cached body layers, reducing the old 694 tile draws per body frame.
The original particle renderer handles disperse/reform and the final collapse.

The original hand and foot positions still determine hits and projectiles.
Smoke damage and slowing, three sharp caps, cooldowns, jump height, difficulty
timing, and the fourth-hit dodge retain their existing rules.

PLAYTEST
Open atomdust-review.html through your normal game server/site. Choose Summy,
Sadie, or Vee, then Practice or Fight Atomdust. Move buttons, quarter speed,
and Face right help inspect the attacks and disperse/reform. Practice protects
against smoke as well as ordinary attacks; Fight restores normal damage.
Original Atomdust compares the previous animation.

Normal play opens through index.html or hero-showdown.html. Story Mode remains
available. The separate playable-villains competitive branch is not included.
The owner continues to handle game uploads and publication personally.
