HERO SHOWDOWN — BUSHMAN COMBINED MANUAL UPDATE
October 26, 2026

This cumulative package includes Bushman, Lightening Spree, Atomdust,
Venomis & Thornbear, Sonic Tusk, Styrodog, Dr. Styro, Wastelander, and
the saved Super Summy, Super Sadie, and Super Vee animation work.
Use this ZIP in place of the earlier animation-update ZIPs.

INSTALL WHEN YOU ARE HOME
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Copy the CONTENTS of GAME-FILES into your existing game folder, beside
   index.html. Keep the assets/animation folder structure. Replace matching
   files and keep all other existing game files.
3. Upload the files yourself, then reload without old cached files.

There are 65 game files: 53 production files and twelve optional review
files. README.txt and DEVELOPMENT contain notes and checks for reference.

BUSHMAN
Thirty-two new full-body poses add an eight-step walk, planted idle and
guard, separate light/heavy reactions, and preparation/contact/recovery
for his punch, kick, fireball, burning-ground cast and crossed-arm blast.
His arms stay crossed through the signature blast release.

The original attack timing, hit detection, fire effects, four-hit charge,
double blast damage, co-op state, counter and kneeling defeat are preserved.
New body poses wait for the actual attack release. Missing new artwork
automatically falls back to the complete original animation.

PLAYTEST
Open bushman-review.html through your normal game server/site.
Choose Summy, Sadie, or Vee, then Practice or Fight Bushman.
Move buttons, quarter speed and Face right help inspect the sequences.
Hold contact freezes a move after its actual release; uncheck to continue.
Starting Practice or Fight clears that hold. Fight uses normal damage.
Original Bushman compares the earlier art.

Normal play opens through index.html or hero-showdown.html. Story Mode
remains available. Air Pollution Monster is unchanged in this checkpoint.
The separate playable-villains competitive branch is not included.
The owner handles all game uploads and publication personally.
