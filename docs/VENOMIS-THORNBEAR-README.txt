HERO SHOWDOWN — VENOMIS & THORNBEAR COMBINED MANUAL UPDATE
October 26, 2026

Use this ZIP in place of the earlier Dr. Styro, Styrodog and Sonic Tusk update
ZIPs. It includes their saved work plus Venomis & Thornbear. This is a
changed-files update for your existing game, not a standalone full game.

INSTALL WHEN YOU ARE HOME
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Copy the CONTENTS of GAME-FILES into your existing game folder, beside
   index.html. Keep the assets/animation folder structure. Replace matching
   files and keep all other existing game files.
3. Upload those files yourself. The observed repository has index.html and
   hero-showdown.html at its root.
4. Reload without old cached files after replacing the game files.

46 game files are included: 40 production files and six optional review
files for Styrodog, Sonic Tusk, and Venomis & Thornbear. README.txt and
DEVELOPMENT contain notes and source checks, not game uploads.

VENOMIS & THORNBEAR
48 selected drawings: 32 for Thornbear and 16 for Venomis, with eight walking
poses for each, breathing idle poses, bear hit reactions, and scientist
commands. Thornbear has claw windup/contact/recovery, three flytrap releases,
and poses that follow the ground vines' actual age. Venomis keeps walking
when catching up, including during the bear's attacks and hitstun; she gives
commands when stationary. Four transparent source PNGs provide the selected
drawings.

Existing damage, collision rules, difficulty timing, follower behavior,
co-op hit flags, effects, and both original defeat sequences are preserved.
Both actors fall back to their original animation if any new atlas is missing.

PLAYTEST
Open venomis-thornbear-review.html through your normal game server/site after applying the files. Choose Summy, Sadie,
or Vee, then Practice or Fight Venomis & Thornbear. Move buttons, quarter
speed, and Face right help inspect each action. Original Venomis & Thornbear
compares the previous animation. Normal play opens through index.html or
hero-showdown.html.

INCLUDED EARLIER WORK
Supergirls, Wastelander, Dr. Styro, Styrodog, and Sonic Tusk animation files
are included. Story Mode remains available. The separate playable-villains
competitive branch has not been merged into this animation package.
