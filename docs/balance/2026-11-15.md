# Royale combat and progression balance — 15 November 2026

Baseline: `7fec8c40389b9196d2f3507bccb337a99848660a`.

## XP rollback requested after playtesting

The kill-reward pacing and high-enemy-level carry penalty described below were reverted on November 15. Solo kills again use the baseline level-gap multiplier, and local co-op again divides the full ordinary monster/boss XP pool equally with alternating odd remainders. For example, a level-66 wisp again grants 1,160 XP to a level-66 solo hero rather than 34. The next-level requirement remains 18,770 XP.

Existing levels, XP, Power Points and save formats are unchanged. Combat improvements, the 2% defeat penalty, and summoned-helper reward exclusion remain. The original measurements below document the superseded pacing experiment, not the current leveling rate.

## What was measured

This is a source audit and deterministic simulation of the production game, not recorded player analytics. The audit executes the production import map, real attack/contact timers, enemy AI, cooldowns, damage, and legal earned skill points. It covers all 21 normal enemy types, seven regions, day/night, and groups of 1, 3 and 8. There are 354 combat scenarios per audit, plus entry-level pressure and save regressions. Results are in the adjacent baseline and after JSON files.

Offense tests pin enemies in range and disable retaliation, so they are ideal-contact lower bounds. Active encounters pursue the nearest enemy and hold kick/powers without dodging, healing or gear bonuses. Kills cannot level up/refill the simulated hero. This separates combat safety from XP rewards; it is not a skilled human playthrough. Actual positioning, aim, skill investment, gear and healing change results.

Reproduce: `node --import ./tools/release-build/register.mjs tools/release-build/game-balance-audit.mjs`.

## Changes

- Shared 0.8-second protection after a damaging hit prevents simultaneous enemies from stacking lethal damage. Existing per-attack deduplication and boss recovery remain. Protection is independent for each local co-op hero.
- Regular enemy damage in regions 2–7 is 85% of the former daytime value. Night in regions 3–7 is now 1.2× day, previously 2×. Boss base damage, attack warnings and HP are unchanged.
- Basic punch growth is 1.4 damage per hero level; kick growth is 2.0, previously 0.18/0.25. Level-1 basics, attack timing, reach and armor are unchanged.
- Ready melee powers can take over after a held basic attack finishes its contact window. Previously the held-basic chain could block the superpower indefinitely. Existing power combos and early basic contact cannot be cancelled this way.
- Future monster and boss XP uses a gradual earned-reward curve. Saved XP and level thresholds are unchanged. Enemies up to seven levels above the hero retain the normal challenge factor; much higher enemies receive a smooth carry penalty. Existing overlevel reductions remain.
- Local co-op divides one eligible, level-adjusted XP pool equally, alternating odd remainders. It previously substituted raw enemy XP and bypassed level reductions. The least eligible hero still leveling sets the shared pool, discouraging high/low-level carries while retaining equal shares and individual coin rewards. A level-99 companion does not zero the growing hero's pool.
- Summoned boss helpers grant no XP, coins or drops, closing repeat-wave farming.
- Defeat costs at most 2% of next-level XP, previously 10%. The death-screen text agrees.

Animal Allies and Seismic Smash retain their six-second cooldowns, evolution/target limits, animations, and full-Whirlwind damage equality at every skill rank. Health caps, existing XP, levels, Power Points, monster saved HP/IDs, outfit prices, coins, appearances, saves and cloud schemas are preserved. No checkpoint healing or online multiplayer is introduced.

| Level 66 vs 3 phantoms at night, classic powers | Dead at 2.37 s | All 3 defeated at 8.05 s; 377 / 1,075 HP |
| Level 66 vs 3 level-80 sentinels at night, classic powers | Dead at 0.67 s | Still dangerous: dead at 9.30 s |
| Same sentinels, crowd-control build | Dead at 7.97 s | All 3 defeated at 17.50 s; 161 / 1,075 HP |
| Level 66 basic-kick-only ideal wisp fight | 59.27 s | 10.27 s |
| Level 99 basic-kick-only ideal titan fight | 203.82 s | 32.35 s |
| Level 99 classic-power ideal titan fight | 30.03 s | 9.22 s |

Every equal-level single-enemy active encounter now finishes without death in this simulation. Late groups can still win against careless melee pursuit. Every region-entry level survives a three-second eight-attacker staggered night burst against all enemy types in that region. Staying in damage eventually kills the hero: this is a reaction window, not immunity.

## Progression model and limits

For each level from 1 to 98, the model uses the best XP reward from normal enemies no more than seven levels higher, divides the unchanged next-level requirement by that reward, and assumes a fixed sustained kill rate. It does not include travel, downtime, mistakes, boss rewards or switching costs. It is a sensitivity model, not a claim about measured average player speed.

| Sustained kills/minute | Estimated hours to level 99 |
|---|---:|
| 8 | 93.20 |
| 12 | 62.14 |
| 15 | 49.71 |
| 20 | 37.28 |
| 30 | 24.85 |

The central target is about 50 active hours at 15 kills/minute. At 30 kills/minute, the model is about 25 hours; slower players can take substantially longer. This is not a hard one-day lock or an anti-cheat guarantee: faster routes, exploits, modified clients or future builds may beat the model. No daily cap, forced wait or reset of existing progress was added.

## Verification

The release gate includes regression coverage for overlapping damage, all region-entry floors, night/day damage, meaningful late basics, held-input powers, level-66 fights, preserved old XP/HP/skill ledgers at all 99 levels, death penalties, helper farming, eligible co-op sharing and per-player protection. Existing power-parity, animation, appearance, cloud-save isolation, slot-3 and controller tests remain in the release workflow. The browser co-op fixture intentionally checks zero XP for its overlevelled coastal heroes; matching-level XP sharing is checked through real kill/reward calls in the production runtime tests.

The packaged preview was opened in a browser, an existing local QA hero resumed, the Tree of Eris and gameplay rendered, and movement/health HUD worked. GitHub Actions must pass the complete production, starter-suit, four-power and browser co-op suites before Pages deployment.
