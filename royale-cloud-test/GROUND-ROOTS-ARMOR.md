# Ground Roots armor

The Tree of Eris wardrobe sells 27 Ground Roots pieces. Each helmet, cuirass and pair of boots has front, profile and rear sprites. Both heroes share the equipment art and use their own Style C body and motion anchors. The hairstyle and cape choices stay in the saved appearance.

A helmet costs a quarter of the set price, chest armor costs half and boots cost the remainder. For the 1,250-coin set this gives 312, 625 and 313 whole coins. A set occupies three of the seven inventory slots. Resale pays 25% of the individual item's catalog value rounded down.

An already learned Ground Roots gains the bonus only while all three eligible pieces of one tier are equipped. Power Points and permanent levels stay in the save. Level 20 deals 44 damage per squeeze before other equipment modifiers. Each effective level above 20 adds two damage. At level 29 this gives six 62-damage squeezes against up to five grounded targets, followed by the ordinary ten-second cooldown. Bosses retain the existing movement slow and can attack. Airborne enemies cannot be caught or squeezed. Equipment refresh updates an active cast without restarting its clock or acquiring new targets.

For the wardrobe review, serve the repository and open `royale/tests/fireball-review.html?collection=roots`. Select a tier, rotate either default hero, toggle the cape, or launch a temporary patrol or purchase session.
