# Super Strength armor

The Tree of Eris wardrobe sells nine complete gold suits. Every helmet, chest piece and boot has front, profile and rear artwork. The cuirass supplies a matching pair of gauntlets that follow the elbow and wrist. Separately equipped gloves take precedence. Gauntlets supplied by the cuirass use no additional inventory space.

The helmet receives one quarter of the set price rounded down, the chest receives half and the boots receive the remainder. Fulcrum splits into 312, 625 and 313 coins. Resale pays 25% of the original individual price rounded down. The existing seven-piece inventory includes worn items.

Learn Super Strength and wear all three pieces of the same eligible tier to receive the set bonus. Learned levels and Power Points stay in the save. Removing any piece removes the full bonus, including a pending hit during windup if the inventory refreshes. Above effective level 20, each level adds eight damage to the single punch against regular enemies and bosses. Boss effectiveness caps at 100% at level 20. Reach stays at 59, the target cap remains five, and the normal cooldown is eight seconds. A level-90 hero with 20 learned levels deals 573 damage without other modifiers and 645 in Solar Colossus.

To review the collection in a temporary hero slot, serve the repository and open `royale/tests/fireball-review.html?collection=strength`. Select a tier, choose either hero, toggle the cape for rear plating, or launch a patrol or a purchase trial.
