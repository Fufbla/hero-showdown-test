# Steel Body Power Armor

At the Tree of Eris, open Costume Wardrobe and select **Steel Body Power Armor**. Buy the complete suit or choose helmet, chest, or boots in the part categories. Open Owned to equip each piece. The [armor review](tests/fireball-review.html?collection=steel) shows the Guardian or Sentinel from the front, back, and side and launches isolated patrol and purchase trials.

A matching helmet, chest and boots add the tier bonus to a learned Steel Body. A partial set carries no defensive stat bonus. The normal level-20 curve remains in effect through effective level 20. Each further effective level adds one percentage point of reduction and 0.25 seconds, ending at 84% for 10.25 seconds at level 29. The 15-second cooldown begins when the effect ends. Knockback immunity uses the existing ability rule.

A wardrobe change during an active cast keeps its elapsed time and immediately recomputes its protection and total duration. If the new duration has already elapsed, protection ends and the full cooldown starts. The game derives the bonus from worn IDs. Learned levels and Power Points stay in the existing save ledger.
