# Super Kick armor

The Tree of Eris wardrobe sells nine three-piece silver suits. The original Style C head and torso anchors carry the helmet and chest. Each greave tracks the knee-to-ankle line independently from the toe cap, including the existing sole-facing contact cel.

All three eligible pieces of one tier boost an already learned Super Kick. Above level 20, each effective level adds two damage to each kick. A level-90 hero with 20 permanent levels deals 148 damage per kick without other modifiers and 166 in Argent Dragon. Both combinations have six kicks. The six-second cooldown begins after the final kick finishes. A living target that leaves reach causes a miss; a defeated or removed target allows the next kick to acquire a nearby opponent.

Equipment refreshes update pending damage and trim excess queued kicks. They preserve the strike already in motion, its target and the recovery clock. Learned levels and Power Points stay in the save. Movement speed, basic attack stats and player hitboxes use the original rules.

Helmet, chest and boots take 25%, 50% and the remainder of the full price, rounded to whole coins. Pivot Step costs 312, 625 and 313 coins. Worn items count toward the seven-piece limit. Resale returns 25% of each individual price rounded down.
