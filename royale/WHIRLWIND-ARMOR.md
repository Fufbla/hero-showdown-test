# Whirlwind armor

The Tree of Eris wardrobe sells nine three-piece Whirlwind suits. Each helmet, chest piece and boot has front, profile and rear artwork. The existing Style C equipment renderer places them on the original head, shoulder, knee and ankle anchors.

A complete eligible set boosts an already learned Whirlwind. Learned levels and Power Points stay in the save. Each level above 20 adds three damage per one-second tick. With no other modifiers, level 20 deals 67 damage each second and effective level 29 deals 94. The six-second duration and five-target limit stay fixed above level 20. At most six ticks can hit a given target in a cast. Regular enemies lift and spin; bosses take damage in place. The twelve-second cooldown begins at release.

An equipment change during a cast recalculates future ticks, releases surplus targets, and ends a cast that has reached its reduced duration. Re-equipping never restarts the tick timer or acquires more targets for that cast.

Helmets cost one quarter of the set price rounded down, chest pieces cost half, and boots cost the remainder. Crosswind costs 312, 625 and 313 coins respectively. A suit uses three of seven inventory spaces. Resale pays 25% of an individual piece’s original price rounded down.

Serve the repository and open `royale/tests/fireball-review.html?collection=whirlwind` to inspect the suits, launch a patrol, or try a purchase in an isolated hero slot.
