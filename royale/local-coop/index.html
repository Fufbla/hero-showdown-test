Connecting controllers & how co-op works

Connect two USB controllers, or pair two Bluetooth controllers in your computer’s settings. Press a button on each controller so the browser detects it. Assign a different controller to each player, choose a saved hero, and select Ready. Standard Gamepad API mappings are required.

Use the left stick to move, A / Cross to jump, X / Square to punch, B / Circle to kick, Y / Triangle to pick up, D-pad up for portals, and Start / Options to pause. Powers use LB, RB, RT, and Back; click the right stick to heal.

Player 1 leads the camera. Player 2 returns to safe ground beside Player 1 after going offscreen. XP is divided equally; odd points alternate. Coins are awarded in full to each eligible hero. Equipment rolls once and each hero can pick up their own copy for 20 seconds.

Pause freezes the world and opens two independent menus. Move your controller stick or D-pad to select, A / Cross to confirm, and left/right to change a selection. Both players must select Ready to resume. Either player can leave; the other continues.

Each hero autosaves every eight seconds. Use Leave Local Co-op before closing. Internet is required for login and cloud saves, while combat runs on this device. Unfinished boss fights restart when loaded again.
