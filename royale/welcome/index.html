Ready for adventure?

  1. Make it your hero. Choose Play Game, get a 10-digit code, and customize a hero in one of your three slots. Keep your code safe.
  2. Explore your world. Use the on-screen controls, a controller, or your keyboard. On the default keyboard layout: arrows move, Space jumps, J punches, K kicks, and 1–3 use your powers.
  3. Team up at home. Local Co-op connects two controllers on one computer. Each player uses their own code and saved hero.

Returning? Enter your code for cloud heroes, or choose My Heroes to open heroes saved on this browser.
