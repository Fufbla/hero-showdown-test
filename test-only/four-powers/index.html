How to test each level

Enter 0000000000, continue the test hero, and open Tree of Eris from Pause. Your test hero starts with every power at level 1 and 88 unspent skill points, enough to upgrade all four to level 20. Use the normal upgrade buttons one level at a time. Reset test skill points returns every power to level 1 and refunds the points whenever you want to repeat a test. The test controls also let you select any of the four powers, levels 1–20, and a slot. Place practice targets, resume, and use your normal power buttons.

Local co-op uses two controllers and two separate test heroes. Both players enter 0000000000. On desktop, use your controllers’ shoulder buttons and right trigger for the three active powers.
